## BatteryLog 2.2.1-pre3 (test build)

Test build on top of 2.2.1-pre2. Your logs and settings are kept.

### HUD overlay
- **Position**: Top / Middle / Bottom and Left / Center / Right, so the HUD can sit top center, bottom center, in the middle or on either side. Your old corner choice carries over. Dragging works from any position.
- **Layout**: Horizontal (one line) or Vertical (one item per row), in place of the "One line" switch.

### Fixes
- **GPU load** on phones where only the `gpubusy` file is readable (Honor Magic 8 Pro): shows 0 % while the GPU is idle instead of "—".

### From 2.2.1-pre1 and pre2
- With root or Shizuku, turning on the HUD allows "Display over other apps" by itself.
- **Settings → Per-app monitoring → Share sensor report**.
- CPU temperature ignores limit-only sensors (no more 105 °C), GPU load from `gpubusy`, and watts / battery logs on phones that report mA / mAh.

### Not yet tested on a device
- The new HUD positions and layout on screen.
- The overlay permission and sensor report through Shizuku on a device we control (Shizuku works on the Honor through a tester).
