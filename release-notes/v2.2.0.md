## BatteryLog 2.2.0

A game HUD that stays on screen, and per-app monitoring without root through Shizuku. Your existing logs and settings are kept.

### HUD overlay
- **Settings → HUD overlay**: a small box of live numbers that shows on top of the games you pick, only while that game is on screen.
- Numbers to choose per game: FPS, CPU %, GPU %, GPU MHz, CPU °, GPU °, Case °, Battery °, Battery %, Watts and Clock. Temperatures follow your °C / °F setting; values turn yellow or red when they get high.
- **Games → Add a game**: pick from your installed apps (games listed first, with search). Tap a game in the list to change its numbers, ✕ to remove it. The **HUD** card on an app's page in the Apps tab does the same.
- Look: corner, size (Small / Medium / Large), background strength, one line or one per row. With **Lock position** off you can drag it anywhere; with it on, touches go through to the game.
- Updates every second while it is shown. Needs **Display over other apps** (the switch asks for it) and per-app monitoring (root or Shizuku).

### Per-app monitoring without root (Shizuku)
- Per-app monitoring and the HUD now also work through [Shizuku](https://shizuku.rikka.app/) on devices without root. Root is used when available, Shizuku otherwise; **Use Shizuku instead of root** forces Shizuku.
- When Shizuku is running but BatteryLog isn't allowed yet, the Apps tab shows **Allow Shizuku**.
- On devices that hide the temperature sensor files, CPU, GPU, case and battery temperatures come from Android's thermal service.
