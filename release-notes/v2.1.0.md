## BatteryLog 2.1.0

Per-app monitoring: see which apps use your battery, how hard they work the CPU and GPU, how hot the device gets while you use them, and how much memory they take. Your existing logs and settings are kept.

### Per-app monitoring (root)
- New **Apps** tab: apps ranked by battery used, screen time, CPU, RAM or GPU memory for Today, 24 h or 7 days, with app names and icons and a live line for the app on screen.
- Tap an app for **one big animated chart** like History, with chips for Power, App CPU, GPU load, CPU temperature, GPU temperature, RAM, GPU memory and CPU (always). Charging periods are shaded; drag to read, pinch to zoom, double-tap to reset.
- Summary per app: screen time, energy (Wh), average and peak watts, GPU and CPU load, temperatures (CPU, GPU, skin, battery) and memory.
- **Show all metrics** (Settings → Per-app monitoring): a min / average / max table of every recorded metric and the raw readings.
- Settings → **Per-app monitoring**: on/off, record every 2 / 5 / 10 / 30 s. Resumes after a reboot or update.
- Recordings are kept in `/sdcard/BatteryLog/apps` (one timeline and one per-app file per day) and follow "Delete logs older than".

How it is measured: watts per app are the whole device's power while that app is on screen; GPU load is device-wide and credited to the app on screen; temperatures come from the device's sensors. CPU time, RAM and GPU memory are truly per app. Needs root.

### Other changes
- One notification for battery logging and per-app monitoring (with **Stop logging** and **Stop apps** buttons).
- History and log pages show **Used** and **Charged** as batteries (for example "2.1 batteries", with 205 % below) when more than one full battery was used or charged.

### Not yet tested on a device
- Per-app monitoring on devices other than the Pocket FIT (GPU readings need an Adreno GPU).
