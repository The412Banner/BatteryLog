## BatteryLog 2.3.0

Your own battery averages: how long a full charge lasts, how long a full charge takes, and how you charge. Your existing logs and settings are kept.

### Your averages (History)
- A new **Your averages** card on the History tab for 7, 30 or 90 days, worked out from your own logs in that range:
  - **Battery lasts** (100 → 0 %) at your average drain
  - **Full charge** (0 → 100 %) and **Top-up** (20 → 80 %), timed per 10 % step, because charging slows down near full. Steps your logs don't cover take the speed of the nearest step that was measured; at least 3 of the 10 steps are needed.
  - **Used per day** and **Charges per day** (counting only days that have logs)
  - Where you usually **plug in** and **unplug**, and your **longest stretch** on battery
  - How many of the 10 charge steps were measured, and how many hours on battery and charging the card is based on
- On 6 h and 24 h the card asks for 7 days or longer.
- History now works out its numbers in the background, so long ranges no longer hold up the screen.

### Now
- While charging, **Full charge 0 → 100 %** appears next to the time to full.
- **Your average** time to full now uses your measured speed for each 10 % step (last 14 days), so it no longer comes out too short near 100 %.

### Tested
- On the Pocket FIT (Android 14, root): installed over 2.2.1, the card appears on History with 9 days of logs ("5d 10h on battery and 2d 23h charging"), no crash.
- Logic tests for the per-step charge time, the per-day numbers and the 3-step minimum.
