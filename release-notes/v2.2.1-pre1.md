## BatteryLog 2.2.1-pre1 (test build)

Test build on top of 2.2.0. Your logs and settings are kept.

### HUD overlay
- With root or Shizuku, turning on the HUD (or **Allow display over other apps**) now allows the overlay by itself. This also works where Android marks "Display over other apps" as a restricted setting for apps installed from an APK file. Without root or Shizuku, Android's settings page opens as before.

### Sensor report
- **Settings → Per-app monitoring → Share sensor report** collects what the phone exposes: every temperature sensor, Android's own temperature list, the GPU files and the battery readings, plus which sensor each BatteryLog reading comes from. It opens the share menu with a text file and also copies the text. Send it to the developer when a reading is missing or wrong on your phone.

### Not yet tested on a device
- The overlay permission on a phone where the setting is restricted (only tested on devices where it was not).
- The sensor report through Shizuku.
